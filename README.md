**Marine Odyssey**

A small arcade-style underwater survival game where you control a submarine and try to survive whatever the ocean throws at you.

**How to play**

Move the submarine with WASD / Arrow Keys or use Cursor Mode from Settings.

Avoid the threats, collect health boosts, and survive as long as you can.

**Features**

1. Two type of submarine controlling that can be changed in the settings.

2. Sharks: They'll follow you and bite your ship.

3. Starfish: They just fly by. But don't try to touch them.

4. Sea mine: Don't get closer, they can explo.... (nevermind)

5. Kraken: It's really mad at you, but with enough practice you can dance with him.

6. Endless (wow)

7. Corals: Sharks can't see you here, but try not to stay for too long, or it will hurt your ship.

**Where the idea came from**

I wanted to make a game where player have to constantly moves and think about what would come next but making levels will feel repetitive.
So i made this endless game where all threats are randomized and it is endless.
